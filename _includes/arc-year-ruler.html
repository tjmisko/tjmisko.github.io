{%- comment -%} Year ruler, shared by every Trajectory grid (projects swimlanes and each waterfall view) so the scale doesn't change when the view switch is used. Every grid here is drawn twice over: once wide, with time running left to right, and once narrow, with time running top to bottom (see "Mobile: the transposed chart" in arc.scss). The two read different scales, so a tick carries both. Wide. Column N carries half-year 2020 + (N-2)/2, so year Y lands on grid line 2 + (Y-2020)*2. The scale starts at 2020 and the little that predates it is clamped to the left edge and marked .is-clipped — there is no room for four more columns at that density. Narrow. Track M carries half-year 2018 + (M-2)/2, so year Y lands on grid line 2 + (Y-2018)*2, carried as --m0. Both narrow charts have room the wide one doesn't, so both start where the work does and neither clips anything: the work chart because a phone is tall and time runs down it, the toolchain matrix because its bars gave up their titles to a name gutter and got four more columns back. Which axis --m0 names is the chart's business — a row on the work chart, a column on the matrix. The two years the wide scale can't afford are emitted as .is-narrow-only: they have no column to sit in over there. Label and rule are separate elements: the rule starts one track in, past the ruler track, because a line drawn the full length would strike through its own label. Both are unpositioned and emitted ahead of the chips, so everything in the chart paints over them without needing a z-index. Every year gets a rule; only even years get a label. Nine labels crowd the wide scale, but the rules are what the eye reads a bar's extent against, so they stay at full resolution. end — the grid line to run the rules down to (one past the last content row). Wide only; narrow rules span the lanes with 1 / -1. last — the last year to label.
