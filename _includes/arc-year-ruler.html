{%- comment -%} Year ruler, shared by every Trajectory grid (projects swimlanes and each waterfall view) so the scale doesn't change when the view switch is used. Every grid here is drawn twice over: once wide, with time running left to right, and once narrow, with time running top to bottom (see "Mobile: the transposed chart" in arc.scss). The two read different scales, so a tick carries both. Wide. Column N carries half-year 2020 + (N-2)/2, so year Y lands on grid line 2 + (Y-2020)*2. The scale starts at 2020 and the little that predates it is clamped to the left edge and marked .is-clipped — there is no room for four more columns at that density. Narrow. Track M carries half-year O + (M-2)/2, where O is narrow_origin, so year Y lands on grid line 2 + (Y-O)*2, carried as --m0. Which axis --m0 names is the chart's business — a row on the work chart, a column on the matrix. The two narrow charts no longer agree on O, which is why it is a parameter. The matrix reaches back to 2018 because its bars do: they gave up their titles to a name gutter and got four columns back, so the first spreadsheets can start where they started. The work chart starts at 2020 with the wide one, because the only thing it had before then was the degree — and the degree is arc.wide_only, a phone having one screen of height for the whole arc and none of it to spend on scene-setting. Two empty years at the head of the chart would be the same waste by another means. Years before the origin are simply not drawn. Wide, where the scale always starts at 2020, the pre-2020 rules exist only for a narrow chart that reaches back that far, so they come out .is-narrow-only — they have no column to sit in over there. Label and rule are separate elements: the rule starts one track in, past the ruler track, because a line drawn the full length would strike through its own label. Both are unpositioned and emitted ahead of the chips, so everything in the chart paints over them without needing a z-index. Every year gets a rule; only even years get a label. Nine labels crowd the wide scale, but the rules are what the eye reads a bar's extent against, so they stay at full resolution. end — the grid line to run the rules down to (one past the last content row). Wide only; narrow rules span the lanes with 1 / -1. last — the last year to label. Pass the build year so the scale grows with the calendar; pass a literal only for a grid whose columns are fixed. narrow_origin — the first year on the narrow scale.
