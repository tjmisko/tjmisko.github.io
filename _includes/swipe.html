{%- comment -%} Horizontal swipe for touch screens, shared by the portfolio rolodex and the skills tabs. Each gesture is locked to one axis within its first few pixels: sideways, and the page is held still for the rest of it, so a swipe with a little vertical drift turns the tabs and nothing else; steeper, and the gesture is the page's scroll and never counts as a swipe. Touch events, not pointer events: a browser that decides a touch might be a scroll cancels its pointer events on the spot, and iOS decides that about most touches that start on text — so a sideways swipe across the skills only registered after a tap had settled things. Touch events run to the end of the gesture whatever the browser does with it. The lock has to come before the browser starts scrolling: once it has, its touchmoves are no longer cancelable and the page would move as well. A gesture the browser has already taken is left to it. {%- endcomment -%}
