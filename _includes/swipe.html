{%- comment -%} Horizontal swipe for touch screens, shared by the portfolio rolodex and the skills tabs. Only a sideways flick counts; anything steeper is left to the page's own scroll. Touch events, not pointer events: a browser that decides a touch might be a scroll cancels its pointer events on the spot, and iOS decides that about most touches that start on text — so a sideways swipe across the skills only registered after a tap had settled things. Touch events run to the end of the gesture whatever the browser does with it, and the slope test below is what tells a swipe from a scroll. {%- endcomment -%}
