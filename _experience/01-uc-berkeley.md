Three courses stayed with me.

* **Economic History of the Twentieth Century** (ECON 115, with Brad DeLong): Taught from a preprint of *Slouching Towards Utopia*, the course covered the long twentieth century through economics and technology.
* **Applied Econometrics and Public Policy** (Econ C142, with David Card): The course combined econometrics and machine learning methods, with rigorous theory work and paper replication assignments.
* **Real Analysis** (MATH H104, with Charles Pugh): I got solid foundations in real analysis and learned that I liked mathematics but was not going to be a mathematician.

I also took coursework beyond my degree in CS, AI, and statistics.

* **Programming, Data Structures, and Operating Systems** (CS 61A/B/C).
* **Artificial Intelligence** (CS 188).
* **Probability Theory** (STAT 134) and **Statistics** (STAT 135).
