* **What it is.** Two concurrent B.A. degrees — Applied Mathematics and Economics, the latter with honors — finished in May 2022. The honors track ended in a thesis estimating which national air-quality policies actually moved measured pollution across the OECD.
* **Why it matters.** The pairing is why the research and the engineering read as one line of work rather than a career change: the mathematics is what makes the econometrics defensible, and the systems coursework is what made building the platforms around it possible.

**Coursework that carried forward**

* Programming, Data Structures, and Operating Systems (CS 61A/B/C) — the systems grounding under every tool built since
* Artificial Intelligence (CS 188) — search, inference, and learning
* Probability and Statistics (STAT 134/135) — the inference theory the later regression work rests on
