I returned to IRLE in July 2024 to lead the software and data engineering efforts behind <a class="project-link" data-project="04-sspi-data-webapp" href="#projects-card">sspi.world</a>, which supported the collection, analysis, and presentation of the expanded SSPI dataset. I led the design and development of the Flask and MongoDB application---frontend, backend, data pipeline, and deployment---while also leading the research teams and deciding research direction with the project's PI. The platform replaced a manual Google Sheets process with pipelines over 183 datasets from 25 statistical organizations.
