A working paper on whether county-level home prices fall when wildfire smoke worsens local air quality. I matched roughly 28 million NOAA satellite smoke observations to about 98,000 Zillow home prices at the county-month level, and the estimates were null.

* I built a causal regression framework in R for the effect of air-quality shocks on housing prices.
* The regressions include controls and adjust for spatial autocorrelation, and I ran sensitivity tests across specifications.
